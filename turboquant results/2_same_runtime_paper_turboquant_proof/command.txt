2. Same-runtime paper TurboQuant proof

Tests:
- baseline and turbo hosts
- baseline runs f16 and marks unsupported tq rows explicitly
- turbo f16 vs turbo tq25 vs turbo tq35
- same-runtime causal speed and live-KV benefit comes from turbo rows
- headline speed rows are full-GPU only
- prefill-heavy only
- extra-lite matrix for about 15 to 30 minutes on 2x Tesla M40

docker compose -f /srv/share/ollama-benchmark/ollama-main-turboquant/docker-compose.ollama-bench.yml exec ollama-bench kvstress-bench \
